Start with the role that matches what you want to do. You can read project and launch information before connecting an account.

## Launch an asset

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose a launch format" id="choose-a-launch-format">

[Compare the three formats](/launches/overview). Decide whether you need continuous price discovery, competitive bids, or a fixed-price raise.

</Step>
<Step title="Prepare the asset and project" id="prepare-the-asset-and-project">

Prepare your [project profile](/creators/project-profile), asset details, and [supply allocation](/creators/tokenomics).

</Step>
<Step title="Define the economic terms" id="define-the-economic-terms">

Choose XLM or USDC, the opening date, pricing, and team cliff and vesting schedule.

</Step>
<Step title="Set participation rules" id="set-participation-rules">

Decide whether participation is open or subject to [reputation criteria](/creators/eligibility).

</Step>
<Step title="Review and authorize" id="review-and-authorize">

Review the complete configuration and authorize launch creation. The intended flow issues the asset and moves the launch supply into its vault.

</Step>
</Steps>

The current factory starts with an **existing Stellar asset**. Integrated asset issuance and the complete launch-creation interface are still planned.

## Participate in a launch

<Steps>
<Step>

Inspect the project, asset issuer, format, price rules, allocation policy, and claim schedule.

</Step>
<Step>

Check the [network and deployment record](/deployments/overview).

</Step>
<Step>

Connect your Stellar account through the interface. Add optional identity connections if the launch requires reputation.

</Step>
<Step>

Fund the quote asset and prepare the required trustlines.

</Step>
<Step>

When the launch opens, buy from the curve, place a bid, or commit to the presale, depending on the available format.

</Step>
<Step>

Follow the [allocation and claim guide](/participants/allocations-and-claims).

</Step>
</Steps>

A start date opens participation. It does not mean every asset is immediately transferable or that secondary-market liquidity exists.

## Build an integration

Read the [architecture](/developers/architecture), then use the [contract references](/developers/launch-contracts) or [reputation client](/developers/reputation-contract). The API, SDK, and natural-language CLI pages describe planned interfaces and their current availability.

Use [implementation status](/getting-started/status) to distinguish source support from a deployed service.
