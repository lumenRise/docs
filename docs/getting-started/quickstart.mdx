Start with the role that matches what you want to do. You can read project and launch information before connecting an account.

## Launch an asset

1. [Compare the three formats](/launches/overview). Decide whether you need continuous price discovery, competitive bids, or a fixed-price raise.
2. Prepare your [project profile](/creators/project-profile), asset details, and [supply allocation](/creators/tokenomics).
3. Choose XLM or USDC, the opening date, pricing, and team cliff and vesting schedule.
4. Decide whether participation is open or subject to [reputation criteria](/creators/eligibility).
5. Review the complete configuration and authorize launch creation. The intended flow issues the asset and moves the launch supply into its vault.

The current factory starts with an **existing Stellar asset**. Integrated asset issuance and the complete launch-creation interface are still planned.

## Participate in a launch

1. Inspect the project, asset issuer, format, price rules, allocation policy, and claim schedule.
2. Check the [network and deployment record](/deployments/overview).
3. Connect your Stellar account through the interface. Add optional identity connections if the launch requires reputation.
4. Fund the quote asset and prepare the required trustlines.
5. When the launch opens, buy from the curve, place a bid, or commit to the presale, depending on the available format.
6. Follow the [allocation and claim guide](/participants/allocations-and-claims).

A start date opens participation. It does not mean every asset is immediately transferable or that secondary-market liquidity exists.

## Build an integration

Read the [architecture](/developers/architecture), then use the [contract references](/developers/launch-contracts) or [reputation client](/developers/reputation-contract). The API, SDK, and natural-language CLI pages describe planned interfaces and their current availability.

Use [implementation status](/getting-started/status) to distinguish source support from a deployed service.
