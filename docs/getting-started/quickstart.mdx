Start with the role that matches what you want to do. You can read project and launch information before connecting a wallet.

Base is the live network. Stellar is the expansion.

## Launch a token

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose a launch format" id="choose-a-launch-format">

[Compare the three formats](/launches/overview). Decide whether you need continuous price discovery, competitive bids, or a fixed-price raise. Bonding curves, presales, and auctions all run on Base.

</Step>
<Step title="Prepare the token and project" id="prepare-the-asset-and-project">

Prepare your [project profile](/creators/project-profile), token details, and [supply allocation](/creators/tokenomics). On Base that token is an ERC-20. On Stellar it is a Stellar asset.

</Step>
<Step title="Define the economic terms" id="define-the-economic-terms">

On Base, quote in USDC. On Stellar, choose XLM or USDC. Set the opening date, pricing, and team cliff and vesting schedule.

</Step>
<Step title="Set participation rules" id="set-participation-rules">

Decide whether participation is open or subject to the launch's access rules. On Stellar you can attach [reputation criteria](/creators/eligibility).

</Step>
<Step title="Review and authorize" id="review-and-authorize">

Review the complete configuration and authorize launch creation. The factory deploys the vault and moves the launch supply into it.

</Step>
</Steps>

Confirm you are on Base before signing a production launch. Copy the factory address from [Base factory](/deployments/base).

## Participate in a launch

<Steps>
<Step>

Inspect the project, token, format, price rules, allocation policy, and claim schedule.

</Step>
<Step>

Check the [network and factory record](/deployments/overview). Production volume is on Base.

</Step>
<Step>

Connect the wallet for that network. Base uses an EVM wallet. Stellar uses a Stellar account through the interface.

</Step>
<Step>

Fund the quote asset. On Base that is USDC, plus ETH for gas. On Stellar, hold XLM or USDC and the required trustlines.

</Step>
<Step>

When the launch opens, buy from the curve, place a bid, or commit to the presale.

</Step>
<Step>

Follow the [allocation and claim guide](/participants/allocations-and-claims).

</Step>
</Steps>

A start date opens participation. It does not mean every token is immediately transferable, or that a secondary market already exists.
