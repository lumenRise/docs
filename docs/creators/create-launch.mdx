---
title: "Create a launch"
description: "Configure the project, token, allocations, pricing, vesting, and access rules."
---

The creation flow turns a complete project configuration into a scheduled launch. Prepare the economic terms before authorizing any token issuance or transfer.

Base is the live network. Bonding curves, presales, and auctions all run there. Stellar is the expansion; its factory currently exposes bonding-curve creation.

## Prepare your project

Choose the project name, token symbol, supply, description, logo, website, team members, and relevant links. Explain the product, the use of raised funds, and the purpose of the token. See [project profiles](/creators/project-profile).

Use a distinct distribution wallet to hold the supply intended for the vault. The current factory's `owner` is the distribution account and the recipient for team claims.

## Select the format

Choose a [bonding curve](/launches/bonding-curves), [auction](/launches/auctions), or [presale](/launches/presales). Format-specific pricing and settlement fields should only appear for the selected format.

On Base, all three formats are live. On Stellar, the factory exposes `create_bonding_curve`.

## Set supply percentages

Define what percentage belongs to the sale, future liquidity, and team. The current curve requires all three percentages to add up to 100%, with nonzero pool and sale allocations. The team share can be zero.

Other tokenomics categories can be explained in the project profile, but the current vault does not create extra treasury, campaign, or advisor buckets.

## Choose the quote asset

On Base, the quote is USDC. On Stellar, pick XLM or USDC and verify its network-specific identity. Every price, contribution, target, and quote-denominated fee for this launch uses that asset.

## Set prices and dates

For a curve, choose the initial price, reserve shape, graduation target, creator fee, and intended creator payout share. The initial price and target must correspond to a valid reserve configuration; they are not unconstrained independent numbers.

For an auction, the configuration includes the bidding window and price constraints. For a presale, it includes the fixed price, funding goal, commitment window, and allocation policy.

Use UTC timestamps when reviewing final terms. Current curve trading opens at `starts_at` and has no end date.

## Configure vesting and eligibility

Choose the team's cliff, duration, and release interval. Review the actual first release and final release dates. With both durations set to zero, the team allocation is immediately claimable.

Choose open participation or, on Stellar, an existing [reputation label](/reputation/criteria). A nonzero label must exist in the factory's registry. Presales also need participant vesting and an oversubscription policy.

## Review and launch

The review should identify the token, selected network, full supply plan, vault deposit, allocation percentages, quote asset, prices, fees, dates, vesting, and access rules. It should also distinguish onchain terms from profile information.

The **Launch** action coordinates token setup and vault deployment. Confirm you are signing on Base for a production launch, and copy the factory from [Base factory](/deployments/base).

The Stellar path still requires an already-issued asset and a direct factory invocation.

After creation, verify the returned vault, its recorded configuration, and its deposit. The current Stellar contracts do not expose a launch-parameter editor or cancellation method. Do not assume a mistaken start date, price, or vesting schedule can be changed later.
