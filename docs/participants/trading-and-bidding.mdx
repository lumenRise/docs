---
title: "Trading, bids, and commitments"
description: "Know what each action authorizes and when it becomes final."
---

The launch format determines whether your action buys an asset immediately or creates a bid or commitment for later settlement.

## Buy from a curve

A curve buy requests an exact asset amount. The current contract returns:

| Quote field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `gross` | Curve cost before the creator fee |
| `creator_fee` | Additional fee on the buy |
| `user_amount` | Total quote amount you pay |

Set `max_payment` to the maximum total quote amount you authorize. If the current cost exceeds it, the transaction fails instead of accepting a worse price. A successful buy transfers the asset directly to your account.

A quote is a snapshot. Another trade can change the price before yours executes. Review and simulate close to signing.

## Sell a curve position

You need both the asset balance and a recorded sell-back position for the same address. Request `quote_sell(amount)` and set `min_receipt` to the smallest net quote receipt you accept.

A successful sell reduces your position and returns the current curve proceeds after fees. It can return more or less than your original payment. Sell-back is unavailable before opening or after graduation.

## Place an auction bid

The planned auction flow uses a bid price and quantity. Higher-priced bids have allocation priority. A bid is not an immediate fill, and its payment and cancellation rules must be stated in the auction terms.

Price priority, clearing price, and reputation eligibility are separate concepts. See [auctions](/launches/auctions) for the unresolved settlement choices.

## Commit to a presale

The planned presale flow accepts quote-asset commitments at a fixed price. If demand exceeds the available allocation, reputation is intended to prioritize accepted participation. Final allocation can be smaller than a provisional estimate.

Read the published closing, score-snapshot, refund, and participant-vesting rules before committing.

## Check the transaction result

A wallet signature or submission response is not final execution. Confirm the transaction result and resulting balances or entitlements on the correct network.

If submission status is uncertain, inspect the existing transaction hash before constructing another payment. Repeatedly submitting new transactions can create repeated purchases.
