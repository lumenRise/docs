You can browse the interface without logging in. Participation requires an authenticated Stellar account and the authorization needed to move its assets.

## Read the launch terms

Check the project, team, asset code and issuer, network, format, quote asset, pricing, allocation, fees, dates, and claim schedule. Look for the distinction between immediate delivery and a vesting entitlement.

Use [deployments](/deployments/overview) to check the deployment record. Prototype listings and illustrative balances are not proof of a live sale.

## Connect your account

The interface uses Blux for authentication and a Stellar account. The account can be created or connected through the supported login flow. Its `G...` address identifies the participant for positions and reputation.

Optional X and GitHub connections support the intended reputation flow. The current interface can simulate these connections and scores; those preview numbers are not onchain eligibility evidence.

## Meet the entry criteria

Open launches do not require a reputation label. Gated launches require the selected rule to pass for your address at the point of purchase.

Read the criteria rather than judging eligibility by a profile badge. A high social score cannot satisfy a rule requiring both GitHub and onchain thresholds unless those also pass.

## Prepare funds and trustlines

Have enough of the launch's quote asset to cover the amount and any creator trading fee, plus XLM for network costs. For issued assets, make sure the required receiving trustlines exist and have sufficient capacity.

The current contracts do not set up trustlines for you. Never send funds directly to a vault as a substitute for a purchase or contribution method.

## Participate when it opens

For a bonding curve, request a fresh quote and authorize the buy with a payment limit. For a planned auction, submit a bid during its window. For a planned presale, commit under its terms and wait for final allocation.

See [trading and bidding](/participants/trading-and-bidding) and [allocations and claims](/participants/allocations-and-claims).
