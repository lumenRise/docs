You can browse the interface without logging in. Participation requires a connected wallet and the authorization needed to move its tokens.

Base is the live network. Connect an EVM wallet, hold USDC, and keep ETH for gas. Stellar participation uses a Stellar account and is part of the expansion.

## Read the launch terms

Check the project, team, token, network, format, quote asset, pricing, allocation, fees, dates, and claim schedule. Look for the distinction between immediate delivery and a vesting entitlement.

Use [deployments](/deployments/overview) to check the factory record. Production volume is on Base.

## Connect your account

On Base, connect the EVM wallet that will sign buys, bids, or commitments. Its `0x` address identifies the participant for positions.

On Stellar, the interface uses Blux for authentication and a Stellar account. Its `G...` address identifies the participant for positions and reputation.

Optional X and GitHub connections support the intended Stellar reputation flow. Locally simulated scores are not onchain eligibility evidence.

## Meet the entry criteria

Open launches do not require a reputation label. Gated Stellar launches require the selected rule to pass for your address at the point of purchase.

Read the criteria rather than judging eligibility by a profile badge. A high social score cannot satisfy a rule requiring both GitHub and onchain thresholds unless those also pass.

## Prepare funds

Have enough of the launch's quote asset to cover the amount and any creator trading fee, plus gas.

On Base that is USDC plus ETH. On Stellar, hold XLM or USDC and make sure the required receiving trustlines exist.

The contracts do not set up trustlines or token approvals for you. Never send funds directly to a vault as a substitute for a purchase or contribution method.

## Participate when it opens

For a bonding curve, request a fresh quote and authorize the buy with a payment limit. For an auction, submit a bid during its window. For a presale, commit under its terms and wait for final allocation.

See [trading and bidding](/participants/trading-and-bidding) and [allocations and claims](/participants/allocations-and-claims).
