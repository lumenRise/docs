Lumen Rise brings asset launches and reusable reputation to Stellar. Projects choose how to raise, how to distribute their asset, and who can participate. Participants build a reputation they can use across launches and other applications.

:::info[Product and implementation]
Bonding curves, team vesting, and the administered reputation registry exist in contract source. Auctions, presales, production scoring, API, SDK, and the launch CLI are planned. No deployment of the current source version is verified. [Check implementation status](/getting-started/status) before integrating.
:::

## How Launchpad works

A creator defines the asset, supply allocation, XLM or USDC quote asset, opening date, pricing, and vesting terms. The launch holds the allocated supply in a dedicated Soroban vault. Participation follows the selected format; team assets unlock through a separate vesting schedule.

<SupplyFlow />

The product uses **Stellar assets**, identified by their code and issuer. The Stellar Asset Contract gives the vault access to the same asset on Soroban. It does not create a separate wrapped asset.

## Three ways to launch

| Format | Pricing and participation | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bonding curve](/launches/bonding-curves) | A reserve formula changes the price as assets are bought and sold. Trading opens at the start date and continues until graduation. | <Badge status="experimental">Contract prototype</Badge> |
| [Auction](/launches/auctions) | Participants submit bids; higher-priced bids receive allocation priority. Bid during the auction window, then settle the result and claim. | <Badge status="planned">Planned</Badge> |
| [Presale](/launches/presales) | The creator sets a fixed sale price. Commit quote assets, receive a final allocation, and claim under the published vesting terms. | <Badge status="planned">Planned</Badge> |

## Reputation that travels

Connect a Stellar account, X, and GitHub to establish three separate signals: **onchain**, **social**, and **GitHub** reputation. Projects can use these signals to define access criteria or prioritize allocations. Other applications can reuse the same reputation through onchain checks, with API and SDK access planned.

[Understand reputation](/reputation/overview) or [define custom criteria](/reputation/criteria).
