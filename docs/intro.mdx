LumenRise is live on Base.

The launchpad supports bonding curves, presales, and auctions. The Base app is in production, the contracts are audited, and the launchpad has done more than $10k in volume.

We are expanding to Stellar next. Same product, same launch formats, second network.

:::info[Where to launch]
Base is the live network. Use it for production launches. Stellar is the expansion: factory and reputation contracts are documented here, and that network is still being brought up to the Base feature set.
:::

## How Launchpad works

A creator picks a network, a launch format, the token, supply split, quote asset, opening time, pricing, and vesting. The factory deploys a dedicated vault and locks the launch supply there. Participants then trade, bid, or join the presale under the published terms.

<SupplyFlow />

On Base the launch token is an ERC-20 and the quote asset is USDC. On Stellar the launch token is a Stellar asset, reached from contracts through the Stellar Asset Contract, with XLM or USDC as the quote.

## Three ways to launch

| Format | Pricing and participation | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bonding curve](/launches/bonding-curves) | A reserve formula changes the price as tokens are bought and sold. Trading opens at the start date and continues until graduation. | <Badge status="available">Live on Base</Badge> |
| [Auction](/launches/auctions) | Participants submit bids. Higher-priced bids get allocation priority. Bid during the window, settle, then claim. | <Badge status="available">Live on Base</Badge> |
| [Presale](/launches/presales) | The creator sets a fixed price. Commit quote tokens, take a final allocation, and claim under the published vesting terms. | <Badge status="available">Live on Base</Badge> |

Stellar launches use the same three formats as that network comes online. The Stellar factory source is farthest along on bonding curves.

## Reputation that travels

On Stellar, connect a Stellar account, X, and GitHub to build **onchain**, **social**, and **GitHub** reputation. Projects can use those signals for access rules or allocation priority. Other apps can reuse the same registry.

This is part of the Stellar expansion. Base launches can still use the access rules you publish on the launch page.

[Understand reputation](/reputation/overview) or [define custom criteria](/reputation/criteria).
