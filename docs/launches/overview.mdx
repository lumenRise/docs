---
title: "Choose a launch format"
description: "Compare bonding curves, auctions, and presales."
---

All three formats start with the same project information, Stellar asset identity, supply plan, quote asset, and participation rules. The format determines how a participant gets a price and an allocation.

| | Bonding curve | Auction | Presale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing | Changes with the curve's sold supply | Determined through bids | Fixed by the creator |
| Participant action | Buy or sell against the curve | Submit a price and quantity | Commit XLM or USDC |
| Allocation | Executed purchases | Higher-priced bids have priority | Fixed-price allocation, with reputation priority when oversubscribed |
| Opening | At the configured start time | At the auction start | At the presale start |
| Ending | Graduation target reached | Auction close and settlement | Sale close or its published completion rule |
| Participant receipt | Asset transferred during purchase | After settlement, under sale terms | After allocation, under participant vesting terms |
| Source support | Contract prototype | Planned | Planned |

## Bonding curve

Choose a [bonding curve](/launches/bonding-curves) when you want the price to respond to purchases and sell-backs from the opening date. The current curve is formula-driven and has no expiry deadline.

## Auction

Choose an [auction](/launches/auctions) when buyers should express the price they are willing to pay. Higher-priced bids receive priority for a finite asset allocation. The payment rule, closing time, and settlement process must be defined before bidding opens.

## Presale

Choose a [presale](/launches/presales) when you want a known price, a funding goal, and a scheduled distribution. When demand exceeds the available raise, reputation is intended to prioritize final allocations. Unaccepted commitments must remain refundable under the published settlement rules.

## Shared configuration

Creators choose allocation percentages, XLM or USDC, start time, pricing parameters, team vesting, project description, team information, tokenomics, and reputation rules. Format-specific terms belong on the launch page before anyone participates.

A bonding curve is not an auction, and the current prototype does not implement the auction or presale paths. See [implementation status](/getting-started/status).
