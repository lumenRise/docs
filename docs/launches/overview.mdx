---
title: "Choose a launch format"
description: "Compare bonding curves, auctions, and presales."
---

The launchpad supports three formats: bonding curves, presales, and auctions. All three are live on Base. Stellar uses the same formats as that network comes online.

All three start with the same project information, token identity, supply plan, quote asset, and participation rules. The format determines how a participant gets a price and an allocation.

| | Bonding curve | Auction | Presale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing | Changes with the curve's sold supply | Determined through bids | Fixed by the creator |
| Participant action | Buy or sell against the curve | Submit a price and quantity | Commit quote tokens |
| Allocation | Executed purchases | Higher-priced bids have priority | Fixed-price allocation, with reputation priority when oversubscribed |
| Opening | At the configured start time | At the auction start | At the presale start |
| Ending | Graduation target reached | Auction close and settlement | Sale close or its published completion rule |
| Participant receipt | Token transferred during purchase | After settlement, under sale terms | After allocation, under participant vesting terms |
| Base | Live | Live | Live |
| Stellar | Factory and vault in source; Mainnet factory published | Expansion | Expansion |

## Bonding curve

Choose a [bonding curve](/launches/bonding-curves) when you want the price to respond to purchases and sell-backs from the opening date. The curve is formula-driven and has no expiry deadline.

## Auction

Choose an [auction](/launches/auctions) when buyers should express the price they are willing to pay. Higher-priced bids receive priority for a finite token allocation. The payment rule, closing time, and settlement process must be defined before bidding opens.

## Presale

Choose a [presale](/launches/presales) when you want a known price, a funding goal, and a scheduled distribution. When demand exceeds the available raise, reputation can prioritize final allocations. Unaccepted commitments stay refundable under the published settlement rules.

## Shared configuration

Creators choose allocation percentages, the quote asset, start time, pricing parameters, team vesting, project description, team information, tokenomics, and access rules. On Base the quote is USDC. On Stellar it is XLM or USDC.

Format-specific terms belong on the launch page before anyone participates. A bonding curve is not an auction. See [implementation status](/getting-started/status).
