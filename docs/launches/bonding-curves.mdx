---
title: "Bonding curves"
description: "Understand curve pricing, direct trading, sell-back rights, and graduation."
---

A bonding curve quotes the launch asset against a chosen quote asset. Buying increases the amount sold and raises the marginal price. Selling a recorded position back reduces the amount sold and lowers it.

## From opening to graduation

The vault is `Scheduled` before `starts_at`. At that timestamp it becomes `Open`, and eligible participants can buy or sell. There is no end date. Trading continues until a successful buy takes the recorded net quote reserve to or above the graduation target.

At graduation, both curve buys and sells stop permanently. Creator fee claims and team vesting claims remain separate operations.

:::info[Graduation in the prototype]
Pool creation and graduation payouts are not implemented. Reaching the target locks trading; it does not move the reserve into a market. See [lifecycle](/concepts/lifecycle).
:::

## Initial price and curve shape

The creator sets synthetic asset and quote reserves. Their ratio establishes the initial marginal price. Their size relative to the sale allocation determines how sharply the price moves.

These reserves are pricing parameters. They do not represent quote assets deposited by the creator.

For example, with **600 assets** in the sale allocation and virtual reserves of **1,800 assets and 1,800 quote units**:

| Point | Marginal price | Cumulative quote reserve, before fees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before purchases | 1 quote unit per asset | 0 |
| After 300 assets sold | 1.44 quote units per asset | 360 quote units |
| After 600 assets sold | 2.25 quote units per asset | 900 quote units |

These are illustrative parameters. Marginal price is the price of the next small increment, not the average price for a large purchase. Use the contract quote for the full amount.

## Buying

A buy specifies an exact asset amount and `max_payment`, the maximum quote payment including the creator fee. The transaction transfers the quote payment into the vault and the purchased asset directly to the buyer.

Every gated buy checks the buyer's current reputation. A quote does not reserve a price, inventory, or eligibility.

## Selling back

The vault records how much each address purchased directly. That address can sell back up to its recorded position while trading remains open, provided it still holds the assets.

Transferring assets does **not** transfer the sell-back position. Team assets and assets received from another holder do not create a curve sell-back right. A sell-back uses the current curve price and normal fees; it does not refund the original payment.

## Graduation accounting

The target counts the vault's recorded net quote reserve. Creator trading fees and direct donations do not count. A purchase fills in full and can overshoot the target. A creator's future payout share and platform fee are shown by `graduation_preview`, but that method performs no transfers.

For the formula, methods, and parameter limits, see [launch contracts](/developers/launch-contracts).
