---
title: "Architecture"
description: "How the factory, vault, asset interfaces, and reputation registry connect."
---

The contract architecture keeps launch custody separate from the shared reputation registry. The factory deploys a dedicated vault for each curve launch.

## Components

| Component | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Interface | Public discovery, authentication, and prototype project and identity flows |
| Asset issuer and distribution account | Issue the Stellar asset and hold the supply before deposit |
| Stellar Asset Contracts | Provide Soroban access to the project and quote assets |
| Launch factory | Validate parameters, deploy a vault, transfer the owner's balance, and register the launch |
| Bonding-curve vault | Hold allocated assets, price trades, track positions, enforce purchase criteria, and process team and fee claims |
| Reputation registry | Store administered scores and public immutable labels; evaluate criteria |
| Planned backend | Identity linking, evidence collection, score calculation, and project-profile services |
| Planned API, SDK, and CLI | Application integrations and launch orchestration |

## Creation boundary

The owner authorizes `create_bonding_curve(params)`. The factory verifies both SACs, reads the owner's asset balance, validates the allocation and curve, checks a nonzero reputation label, and deploys a vault with its stored WASM hash.

The newly created vault receives the owner's entire asset balance. The factory checks both the zero owner balance and the exact vault balance increase. A failed invocation rolls back the deployment and transfer effects.

Existing unsolicited balances at a predictable vault address do not increase the recorded launch supply. They have no implemented recovery path.

## Trading boundary

Buyers pay the quote SAC and receive the project asset SAC through the vault. The vault records net reserve, creator fees, and address-specific sell-back positions. Its checks and transfers occur in one transaction.

A quote reads curve state but does not prove eligibility. A gated buy calls the registry selected by the factory. A sell does not call the registry.

## Reputation trust boundary

A factory pins one reputation registry address. Launch creators cannot replace it per launch. Registry administrators can change scores and rotate the admin. Public label creators define immutable thresholds.

The registry's authorization protects score writes. It does not establish that the offchain evidence or scoring model is correct.

## Upgrades and storage

The factory stores a vault WASM hash at construction. Rebuilding local code does not change that hash, and the current contracts have no upgrade method. A new factory or vault version requires a separate deployment and record.

Relevant operations extend storage lifetimes. Long inactivity can still require restoration of archived data. Integrations must distinguish an invocation failure from an absent score or zero position.
