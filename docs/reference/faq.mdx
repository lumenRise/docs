## Does Launchpad create an asset or a custom contract token?

The intended product creates an issued Stellar asset. A SAC gives Soroban access to that same asset. The current factory accepts an existing SAC-backed asset; integrated issuance is planned.

## Can I choose my supply allocation and quote asset?

Yes, the product is designed around creator-selected allocations and XLM or USDC quotes. The current curve enforces pool, curve, and team shares totaling 100%. Its `pair` argument accepts a valid SAC rather than enforcing an XLM/USDC allowlist.

## Does moving the balance into a vault prove a fixed supply?

No. The factory moves the owner's full balance. It cannot query total issued supply across the network or prevent later issuance. Issuance controls need separate verification.

## Can people trade as soon as a launch starts?

Current curve trading opens at `starts_at`. Auction opening enables bids; presale opening enables commitments under their future rules. A vesting schedule or unsettled allocation can delay when a participant actually receives assets.

## Do curve launches expire if they miss a goal?

No. The current curve stays open until graduation and has no deadline or failed state. The older Testnet report describes an earlier version.

## What happens at graduation?

A qualifying buy marks the curve graduated and stops all further curve trades. The current prototype does not distribute the graduation reserve or create a pool. See [lifecycle](/concepts/lifecycle).

## Can I sell assets I received from someone else back to the curve?

Only if your address also has enough recorded direct-buy position and still holds the assets. Ordinary transfers do not transfer sell-back rights.

## Is reputation one number?

The registry stores three numbers: social, GitHub, and onchain. It can also compare their unweighted sum to a threshold. The product's final scoring scale and calculation weights are not published.

## Does connecting an account prove I am verified?

It can establish control of the relevant account through a real verification flow. It does not by itself establish legal identity or a KYC status. Current optional interface connections and scores can be simulated.

## Can anyone create their own criteria?

Anyone can create an immutable threshold label with their signature. Labels start at 1 and are local to their registry. A curve's label value of 0 means no reputation gate.

## Can another application use these scores?

Yes, a Soroban contract can use the shared `ReputationClient` or direct registry calls. HTTP API and packaged application SDK access are planned.

## Do higher scores guarantee a presale allocation?

No guarantee can be made from the current implementation. The product intends to prioritize higher-reputation participants when demand exceeds the accepted raise, but the ranking and settlement contracts are not built.

## Are auctions, presales, and the CLI available?

They are planned. Their pages describe the intended behavior and open decisions. No published CLI installer, API endpoint, SDK package, auction contract, or presale contract is supplied.

## Where are the current contract addresses?

See [deployments](/deployments/overview). The supplied evidence contains older Testnet fixtures and no verified deployment of the current source version or Mainnet release.
