---
title: "Connect your accounts"
description: "Link a Stellar identity with public social and developer profiles."
---

Your Stellar account is the address used by the reputation registry. X and GitHub provide public activity signals that can be associated with that address through the intended verification flow.

## Stellar account

Connect or create the account through the interface's authentication flow. Proving control of a wallet means authorizing the relevant challenge or transaction. Knowing a public address alone does not establish control.

The onchain score is intended to reflect the connected account's activity. It is not a score for a specific issued asset held in that account.

## X

The product intends to verify control of your X account and assess public activity and ecosystem relationships. Relevant relationships include who follows you, whom you follow, and their connection to Stellar or web3.

Connecting X does not by itself guarantee a particular score. Follower count alone is not a published scoring formula.

## GitHub

The product intends to verify control of your GitHub account and assess development work and participation. Relevant evidence includes contributions to projects, collaboration, and participation in the Stellar ecosystem.

The interface also contains an optional GitLab connection. The current registry still has one developer field named `github`, not a separate GitLab score.

## Optional connections

The prototype lets users skip optional identity connections and manage them later in Settings. A launch may still require thresholds that a participant without the relevant evidence cannot meet.

Disconnecting an identity in the interface does not automatically erase a score already written to the onchain registry. That requires a scoring and update policy; current scores have no expiry timestamp or automatic revocation field.

## Current implementation

Blux authentication is integrated into the interface. Some optional provider connections are locally simulated and generate illustrative scores. No production account-linking backend or evidence-verification pipeline is present in the supplied repositories.

Only published registry values can support an onchain check. A locally displayed prototype score is not a signed attestation or an onchain score update.
