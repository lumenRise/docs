## Product Purpose

## Capabilities and Constraints

The full product brief includes three launch formats, asset creation, custom allocations, XLM or USDC quotes, team profiles, tokenomics, vesting, reputation criteria, score-based presale allocation, API, SDK, and an agent-friendly CLI. Current source implements bonding-curve vaults, team vesting, and an administered reputation registry. Other surfaces must be labeled planned. Historical Testnet fixtures are not current deployments. No Mainnet deployment is verified.

## Brand Commitments

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

Explain the full intended product. Separate source implementation from network availability. Use Stellar asset terminology precisely. Make launch conditions and reputation rules inspectable. Do not invent endpoints, addresses, formulas, or live capabilities.
