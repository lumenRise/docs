# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Project teams launching tokens on Base, participants evaluating launches, and developers integrating the factory or the Stellar expansion.

## Product Purpose

Document Launchpad: bonding curves, auctions, and presales on Base, plus the Stellar expansion covering locked allocations, vesting, and reusable social, GitHub, and onchain reputation.

## Operating Context

This Docusaurus site sits beside `../evm-contract`, `../stellar-contract`, and the interface apps. Readers need concepts, task guides, accurate contract references, and network-specific deployment records. Base is the live network. Stellar is the expansion.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The live Base product includes three launch formats, token launches, custom allocations, USDC quotes, team profiles, tokenomics, and vesting. The Stellar factory and reputation registry are documented from source. Historical Stellar Testnet fixtures are not current deployments. The Base factory address lives on its own page so it can be filled in when published.

## Brand Commitments

Use the working name Launchpad / LumenRise and the existing interface identity unless the user supplies a replacement. Use direct professional language and no em dashes.

## Evidence on Hand

The user's product brief, sibling EVM and Stellar contract source, sibling interface product and design documents, historical Stellar Testnet report, and official reference documentation.

## Product Principles

Lead with Base. Keep Stellar as a documented expansion, not as the only network. Make launch conditions inspectable. Do not invent endpoints or factory addresses.
